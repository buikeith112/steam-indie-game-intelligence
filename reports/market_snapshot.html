Steam Indie Market Snapshot

Thousands of indie games launch on Steam every year, and half of them never reach 10 reviews. I wanted to understand what actually distinguishes the ones that succeed in a marketplace that crowded. I chose Steam because it is the largest storefront for PC games, and because its public store data shows review counts, prices and tags for every game, so I could build the dataset myself instead of relying on someone else's. I collected all 65,471 games tagged Indie, loaded them into PostgreSQL, and compared the hits with everything else.

MARKET SHAPE

Attention is extremely concentrated

Sorting the 55,113 indie games launched since 2018 by review count, the top 1% hold 70.7% of all reviews. The bottom half of the market holds 0.4%. Most indie releases are effectively invisible.

QUESTION 2 · GENRE SENTIMENT

Platformers, puzzles and point-and-clicks are reviewed most kindly

Among in-scope games with at least 50 reviews, 60% are rated 80% positive or better. 2D platformers (78%), point-and-click (77%) and puzzle games (74%) clear that bar most often. Incremental, simulation, strategy and RPG games sit near 50%. One plausible reason is that players of systems-heavy genres judge balance and content depth more harshly.

Share of games rated ≥80% positive · genre tags with ≥400 games · dashed line = all indie (60%)

How to read it. Genres are each game's top five user tags, so a game counts toward several genres. Only games with 50+ reviews are rated, which removes noisy tiny samples but also the many games that never found an audience.

QUESTION 3 · PRICING

Games priced $20–29.99 reach 1,000 reviews most often

Of indie games launched 2018–2024, 33% of those priced $20–29.99 became hits, against 1.2% of games under $5. Hit rates climb steadily with price until $30, then fall back to 13%.

Hit rate (≥1,000 reviews) by list price · games launched 2018–2024 · n under each bar

Price is not a lever here. Price mostly signals scope: a $25 game usually has a larger team, budget and marketing push than a $3 one. This shows where successful games sit, not that raising a price creates success. Prices are the current list price, used as a launch-price proxy.

NEW QUESTION · SUPPLY VS DEMAND

The fastest-growing genres have the worst odds

Action-adventure and 2D platformer releases more than tripled between 2018–20 and 2022–24, yet only 2–3% of those games became hits. Story-rich games grew about 10% and convert at 10.6%, the best of any large genre.

NEW QUESTION · LAUNCH CHOICES

Publisher-backed games become hits nearly three times as often

Publisher-backed indie games hit 1,000 reviews 8.9% of the time, against 3.2% for self-published ones. Early Access launches do slightly better than direct launches (6.4% vs 4.1%), and games with a Mac build do better than those without (8.7% vs 3.6%).

Hit rate (≥1,000 reviews) by launch attribute · games launched 2018–2024

Two correlation traps. Steam Deck Verified games hit 24.8% of the time and untested games 0.5%. That does not mean Deck support sells games: Valve tests popular games first, so popularity drives the badge more than the reverse. Publishers also choose which games to sign, so part of their advantage is picking promising games, not only marketing them.

Method notes
  • Universe: released Steam games tagged Indie, listed with Steam's store query API. Steam lists only games on sale today, so games removed before the first crawl are missing and older cohorts look somewhat better than they were. Games removed from now on keep their rows and stay in every rate.
  • Hit = 1,000+ reviews as counted on the Steam store page. Reviews are an exact public count that tracks sales (roughly 30–60 sales per review).
  • Success rates use 2018–2024 launches so every game has had about two years to collect reviews. 2026 launch counts rose sharply in recent months, and many of those games have no reviews yet, so 2026 is excluded from rates.
  • Every chart is an association, not a causal effect.
  • A separate technical appendix covers the database design, data pipeline, SQL queries, refresh process and validation.
